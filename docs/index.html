Subscribe in three steps

A subscription stays connected to the live feed. Downloading and importing an `.ics` file creates only a one-time copy.

STEP 01

Choose and copy

Select a calendar style above and copy its subscription URL.

STEP 02

Open your calendar

On a computer, open Google Calendar’s From URL page ↗.

STEP 03

Paste and subscribe

Paste the URL and choose Add calendar. Google controls how quickly later updates appear.
